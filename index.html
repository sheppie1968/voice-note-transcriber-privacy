Voice Note Transcriber – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Voice Note Transcriber collects no personal data for its developer: audio you choose to transcribe goes directly from your device to the transcription service you select, and nothing is sent to us.

Voice Note Transcriber ("the app") is an Android app developed by Mark Shepherd ("we", "us"). It turns voice notes and other audio files you share with it into text. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies only to the app, not to WhatsApp or any other app you share audio from.

Information the app handles

The app only handles audio you actively share with it or pick from your files. It does not record from your microphone or read your messages, contacts or chats.

When you tap Copy or Share, the transcript goes to your device clipboard or to the app you choose. What happens to it then is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Third-party transcription services

Transcription is done by a third-party service, using an API key that you supply. You choose the service in the app's settings:

  • Groq — Groq Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI — OpenAI Privacy Policy

The audio is sent directly from your device to that service under your own account with them. Their privacy policy and terms govern how they process and retain it. We have no access to your account with them, your audio or your transcripts.

Voice notes can contain other people's voices and personal information. Only transcribe audio you have the right to share with the service you choose.

Storage, security and what we don't do

The app has no servers, user accounts or sign-in. Your API key and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read that storage. If Android backup is on, that storage may be included in your device backup.

The app does not contain:

  • analytics or usage tracking
  • advertising or advertising identifiers
  • crash reporting services
  • location, contacts, camera or microphone access

We do not sell, rent or share any information, because we never receive any.

Permissions

The app requests one permission: Internet, to send audio to the transcription service. It reads an audio file only when you share it with the app or pick it yourself.

Your choices

  • Delete your API key at any time by clearing the field in Settings.
  • Remove all data the app stores by clearing its storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling it.
  • To delete audio or transcripts held by Groq or OpenAI, use the controls or contact details in their privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
